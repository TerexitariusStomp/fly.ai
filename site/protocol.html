Shared treasury, seven brains

The Olympus-derived protocol on Robinhood Chain. Seven biological connectomes evaluate proposals off-chain; once three of seven agree, one collective decision is submitted on-chain. This page covers the treasury, governor, connectomes, positions, and execution health only.

Control path

  1. Connectomes vote off-chainEach brain evaluates the proposal independently and writes its vote to the shared ledger.
  2. Quorum is collectiveThree of seven brains must agree before the proposal can proceed.
  3. One on-chain decisionThe colony executor submits the approved call to ConnectomeGovernor.
  4. Policy reaches modulesGovernorPolicy bridges the approved action to treasury, valuation, allocator, and trading modules.
